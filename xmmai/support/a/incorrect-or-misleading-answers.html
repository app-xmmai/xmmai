The AI gave an incorrect or misleading answer

November 23, 2026

AI assistants can sometimes produce answers that sound confident but are wrong.

This is a known limit of current language models, and it affects every assistant.

Why it happens

The model predicts likely text and does not look facts up unless a tool provides them.

It may not know about very recent events.

It can invent names, numbers, quotes or links that look real.

How to check an answer

  • Compare important facts with a trusted source.
  • Ask the assistant to explain how it reached the answer.
  • Ask it to list what it is unsure about.
  • Open any link yourself before relying on it.

Get better answers

Give clear details such as goal, audience and format.

Paste the exact text or data you want it to use.

Ask for step by step reasoning on math and logic problems.

High stakes topics

Do not rely on the assistant alone for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions.

Always confirm with a qualified professional.

Fixing a wrong answer

Tell the assistant what is wrong and ask it to try again.

Start a new chat if the conversation has drifted off track.

Links and files that do not exist

If the assistant says it sent an email or made a file but you cannot find it, it did not.

It can only write text and the tools shown in the app.

Send us feedback

Tell us about answers that were clearly wrong or harmful.

Include the question and the reply if you can.

Treat the assistant as a helpful draft partner and not as a final authority.
