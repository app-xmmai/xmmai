Troubleshooting

XMM AI won’t load or shows a black screenIf XMM AI opens to a black or empty screen, the cause is almost always a stuck cached file or a weak connection.The app freezes or hangs while startingA frozen start screen means the app began loading but did not finish.

Install and update

Install XMM AI on iPhone and iPadXMM AI installs from Safari and then works like a normal app with its own icon.Install XMM AI on AndroidOn Android, Chrome and other Chromium browsers can install XMM AI as an app.Install XMM AI on Windows, Mac and LinuxYou can install XMM AI as a desktop app from Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers.Get the latest version of XMM AIXMM AI updates itself, but your browser sometimes holds on to old files.

Chat

My messages are not sendingIf you press send and nothing happens, or the reply never starts, check the points below.Replies are slow or stop halfwayReply speed depends on your connection, the length of the answer and how busy the service is.The AI gave an incorrect or misleading answerAI assistants can sometimes produce answers that sound confident but are wrong.Build mode preview is blank or not updatingBuild mode lets you create small apps and pages and preview them inside XMM AI.

Media

Image creation fails or looks wrongXMM AI can create images from your description.I can’t upload images or filesYou can attach images and some files to your messages with the plus button.Voice mode and the microphone don’t workVoice features need microphone access and a supported browser.

Your data

My chats disappearedXMM AI keeps your chats on your device, not on a server account.Delete chats and clear your dataYou are in control of the data XMM AI stores on your device.

Account and settings

Change the language or fix text directionXMM AI supports Deutsch, English, Persian, Arabic and Spanish.The sidebar or layout looks brokenXMM AI adapts to phones, tablets and computers.Your name, username and profileXMM AI asks for a name so it can greet you and personalize replies.Use AI memory and settingsSettings let you shape how XMM AI talks to you.
