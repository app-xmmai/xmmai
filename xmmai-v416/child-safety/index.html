Child Safety

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The safety of children and young people matters to us. This page explains our rules, what we do to protect minors, and how to report a concern.

Zero tolerance. XMM AI does not allow any content that sexualizes, exploits, endangers or abuses children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any request to create, describe, find or share it, whether real, fictional or AI-generated.

1. What is not allowed

  • Sexual or suggestive content involving anyone under 18, in text or images, including fictional, drawn or AI-generated depictions.
  • Grooming, or attempts to contact, lure, manipulate or exploit a minor.
  • Requests for help finding, trading or hiding child sexual abuse material.
  • Content that encourages self-harm, dangerous challenges or other harm aimed at children.
  • Sharing a child’s private information, such as their name, school, address or photos, to harass or endanger them.

2. How XMM AI is designed

  • No public feed and no messaging between users. XMM AI is a one-to-one assistant. People cannot contact, follow or find each other inside the app.
  • No accounts. We do not collect an email address or password, and chats are stored in your browser on your own device (see our Privacy Policy).
  • Safety rules for the AI. The assistant is instructed to refuse sexual content involving minors and other harmful requests, and image creation requests of this kind are refused.
  • Action on misuse. We may block, limit or remove access for anyone who tries to use XMM AI against these rules.

3. Age guidance

XMM AI is not meant for children under 13. Young people aged 13 to 17 should use it with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. AI answers can be wrong, so they are not a replacement for a trusted adult, a doctor or a teacher.

4. Tips for parents and guardians

  • Talk with your child about what they ask AI tools and what they share. Remind them never to give out their full name, address, school, phone number or photos.
  • Use the device’s own parental controls to limit apps, browsers and screen time.
  • Chat history lives in the browser on the device. You can review or clear it from the app settings or the browser’s site data.
  • If a child has seen something upsetting, stay calm, listen, and take screenshots if you need to report it.

5. Report a concern

If you see content or behavior that puts a child at risk, tell us at support@xmm-ai.com. Include what happened, when, and a screenshot if you can. Please do not send or forward the material itself.

When we find child sexual abuse material or a child in danger, we remove access and report it to the authorities as required by law.

In immediate danger? Contact your local emergency number right away. To report child sexual abuse material, use your country’s official reporting line, for example the NCMEC CyberTipline in the United States (report.cybertip.org) or INHOPE hotlines worldwide (inhope.org).
