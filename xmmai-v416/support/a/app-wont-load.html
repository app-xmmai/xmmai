XMM AI won’t load or shows a black screen

November 23, 2026

If XMM AI opens to a black or empty screen, the cause is almost always a stuck cached file or a weak connection.

Work through the steps below in order and stop as soon as the app loads.

Check your connection

XMM AI needs an internet connection to send messages and load the latest version.

  • Open another website to confirm your connection works.
  • Switch between Wi‑Fi and mobile data and try again.
  • Turn off any VPN or content blocker for a minute and reload.

Reload the page

Pull down to refresh on your phone, or press Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac) on a computer.

A hard reload forces the browser to download fresh files instead of reusing old ones.

Try a different browser

Open app.xmm-ai.com in another browser such as Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

If it works there, the problem is limited to your first browser’s cache or extensions.

Disable extensions

Ad blockers and script blockers can stop parts of the app from running.

  • Open the page in a private or incognito window.
  • If it loads, disable extensions one by one to find the culprit.

Clear site data

Clearing site data fixes most stuck screens, but it also removes chats saved on this device.

Read “Delete chats and clear data” first if you want to keep anything important.

  • Open your browser settings and find site data or storage.
  • Search for xmm-ai.com and remove its data.
  • Reload app.xmm-ai.com and sign in again.

Update your browser

Very old browsers may not support everything XMM AI needs, so install the latest version of your browser.

On iPhone and iPad this means updating iOS itself.
