Image creation fails or looks wrong

November 23, 2026

XMM AI can create images from your description.

If an image does not appear or looks off, try the tips below.

No image appears

  • Check your connection and try again.
  • Wait a little longer because images take more time than text.
  • Reload the page and repeat the request.

Be clear in your prompt

Describe the subject, the setting, the style and the colors.

Example: a red bicycle on a beach at sunset, realistic photo.

Wrong details

Image models can struggle with text, hands and exact counts.

Ask for a simpler scene and edit details in a separate step.

Request blocked

Some prompts are not allowed, for example violent, sexual or hateful content.

Rephrase your idea in a safe way.

Save your image

Press and hold the image on a phone, or right click on a computer, and choose Save image.

Image looks blurry

Ask for a high detail or sharp result.

Zoomed in screenshots can also look soft.

Many images in a row

If you create many images quickly you may need to wait a moment.

Aspect ratio

Mention square, wide or tall in your prompt for a different shape.

Copyright

Do not ask for logos or characters that belong to others.

Still no luck?

Tip: keep prompts short and focused on one main subject.

Send us your prompt and a screenshot of the result.
