Contact support and report a bug

November 23, 2026

We read every message sent to our support team.

A clear report helps us help you faster.

How to reach us

Use the Contact support button on any page of the help center.

It opens your email app with our address ready to go.

You can also write to support@xmm-ai.com directly.

Response time

We usually reply within one to two business days.

What to include

  • What you were trying to do.
  • What went wrong and any message you saw.
  • Your device and browser, for example iPhone Safari.
  • Roughly when it happened.
  • A screenshot if you can.

Steps to reproduce

List the steps that cause the problem in order.

Say whether it happens every time or only sometimes.

Feature ideas

Tell us what you would like to see and why it would help you.

We read all ideas even when we cannot answer each one.

Security issues

Write security in the subject line and describe what you found.

Please do not share the issue publicly before we answer.

Never share secrets

Do not send passwords or payment details by email.

We will never ask for them.

Service status

Check status.xmm-ai.com before you write, since a known outage may already be fixed.

Thank you

Your reports make XMM AI better for everyone.
